    What DJ Wala is

    DJ Wala (डीजे वाला) is a free, always-on DJ radio for the songs that fill an Indian dance floor. It plays the club, pub, party and baraat set on a loop — Punjabi pop, Bollywood remixes, dhol-driven wedding numbers, 90s and 2000s retro club tracks, and slower after-hours cuts. There is no app to install, no account to make and no cover charge: open the page and press play.
